# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **Fixed:** with **Show each as four value masses** ticked, the opportunities sheet's captions described the simplified masses instead of the print. They now match the normal sheet.
- **New:** an illustrated [walkthrough](docs/WALKTHROUGH.md) that takes one photograph through every setting, with zone-distribution charts, all generated by the plugin itself.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-10

- **Large images are safe.** The optical average works in memory-bounded bands. On a 48-megapixel scan its peak memory fell from about 1.7 GB to about 250 MB, with identical results. Images too wide for the budget are refused with a clear message instead of risking Photoshop.
- **Esc cancels long operations** (optical average, opportunities sheet, zone ladder). Photoshop shows a progress bar, and a cancelled operation leaves the image exactly as it was.
- **Active selections no longer interfere.** A marquee you had active used to become the mask of the panel's new layers. It's now set aside during each operation and restored afterwards.
- **No lost clicks.** Changing overlays, filters, development or burns while the panel is busy now applies your latest choice instead of dropping it, and double-clicking a heavy action can't start it twice.
- **Clearer messages** when Photoshop is busy with a dialog, a text edit or a transform, or is older than 2024.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-10

First release of the **Visualize First** Photoshop panel.

- **Visualize:** write the print you want and choose low key, full scale or high key. Training mode keeps all measurement hidden until you commit.
- **Predict:** guess zones at Color Sampler markers and estimate the dark share of the frame.
- **Reveal:** 1° spot-meter readings, scoring and bias, a zone histogram, key and range readings, separation shares, and suggestions toward your visualization.
- **Interpret:** six filters (#8, #21, #25, #29, #58, #47), N−2 to N+2 development, two-marker placement that reports the exposure shift and N, and edge and sky burning.
- **Opportunities:** a sheet of 20 interpretations (5 filters × 4 developments), each captioned, with any variant applicable back to the image.
- **Ink:** a value scale calibrated from your ink and paper, an optical-average blur, ink-coverage readouts, and a zone ladder.
- **Overlays:** false-colour zones, textured darks, textured lights, middle, no texture, ink families, four values, and a light/shadow split.
- **Practice log:** session history with miss, bias and dark-share trends, plus CSV export.
