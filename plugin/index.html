Open an image to begin.

Open a photograph or a scanned drawing in Photoshop. The panel follows whichever image is active, and keeps each image's progress separately.

Five steps, about five minutes.

  1. Visualize: decide how the print should feel.
  2. Predict: mark key tones and guess their zones.
  3. Reveal: see what the meter says, and where you missed.
  4. Interpret: filter, place, develop and burn.
  5. Compare: see twenty versions side by side.

The panel only adds layers inside a "Visualize First" group; your own layers are never touched.
